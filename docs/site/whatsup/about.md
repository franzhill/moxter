**Moxter is developed by its creator.**

I am a computer engineer based in the South of France, with over 20 years of experience in software development.

I've worked across a wide range of projects, teams and technical environments. Along the way, I've come to appreciate a few things: sound engineering, simplicity where possible, maintainability over short-term convenience, and finding the right compromise when constraints inevitably come into play.

I'm naturally curious about how things work and enjoy exploring ideas, questioning established practices and building tools that make everyday engineering a little better.

Moxter is one such initiative: a project born out of professional experience, personal convictions and, quite simply, the pleasure of building something useful.

## A little more about me

In my spare time, when not actively coding, tinkering with technology or poking things and ideas for answers, I enjoy playing guitar, roaming the countryside trails on my mountain bike or swimming in lakes whenever I get the chance.
