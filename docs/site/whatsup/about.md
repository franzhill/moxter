Moxter is developed by Franz Hill

I am a computer engineer based in the South of France. After working across a wide range of projects, I’ve come to value quality and the search for the right compromise: doing things well while respecting each project’s constraints.
Moxter grew out of seeing testing too often neglected in software projects. It aims to make reliable testing easier to adopt and maintain.
